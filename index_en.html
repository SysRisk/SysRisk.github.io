01 / ABOUT

About me

I'm a penetration tester and red team consultant with a background in network engineering and systems administration. I've worked with organizations in finance, healthcare, technology, and the public sector to evaluate their defenses under realistic conditions.

My goal on every engagement is simple: give your team a clear picture of where you're exposed, how much it matters to the business, and exactly what to do about it. Findings are only useful if they lead to fixes.

Outside of client work I mentor people new to the field, contribute to community training events, and spend too much time on capture-the-flag challenges.

02 / SERVICES

What I do

PT

Penetration Testing

Scoped testing of external and internal networks, web apps, and APIs to identify exploitable weaknesses and validate their impact.

RT

Red Team Assessments

Goal-based exercises that measure how well your people, processes, and tools detect and respond to a realistic threat.

PU

Purple Team Workshops

Collaborative sessions with your defenders to tune detections, close visibility gaps, and build response muscle memory.

VM

Vulnerability Management Reviews

Assessment of your scanning, triage, and patching processes to make sure the right vulnerabilities get fixed quickly and consistently.

TR

Training

Hands-on workshops for developers and IT teams on secure practices and how real attacks unfold.

AD

Security Advisory

Remediation guidance, retesting, architecture review, and help building a sustainable security testing program.

03 / APPROACH

How an engagement works

Scoping & authorization

We agree on objectives, targets, timing, and rules of engagement in writing before any testing begins.

Assessment

Methodical testing aligned to industry frameworks such as PTES, OWASP, and MITRE ATT&CK, with regular check-ins.

Reporting

A clear executive summary plus detailed technical findings, risk ratings, and prioritized remediation steps.

Debrief & retest

A walkthrough with your team, followed by verification testing once fixes are in place.
